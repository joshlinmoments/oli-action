About the Program

Cultural Exchange, hand in hand 🤝

The Baiyangshan Charitable Foundation, a non-profit based in Taiwan, is guided by the mission of “purifying minds & inspiring cultural reformation.” Together with students from SIST, we form a joint international volunteer team to serve children at local Government Schools in India. Through training, co-creation, and exchange, participants build cross-cultural communication skills, teamwork, and a global perspective — while bringing light and hope to others through practical action.

Core Pillars

Cultural Exchange × Character × Teamwork

🎎

Taiwan Cultural Exchange

Interactive Chinese learning, cultural riddles, and hands-on crafts that build friendship between Taiwanese and Indian youth.

🌱

Volunteer Training

Led by Taiwanese facilitators — team-building, lesson rehearsals, and practical simulations that grow your leadership and spirit of service.

🏫

School Camp Service

Taiwanese and Indian volunteers join hands to visit Government Schools, inspiring children through character games and team challenges.
