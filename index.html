01 — About the Program

Cultural Exchange, hand in hand

A joint international volunteer team formed by youth from Taiwan and students from SIST, serving children at local Government Schools in India.

The Baiyangshan Charitable Foundation, a non-profit based in Taiwan, is guided by the mission of purifying minds & inspiring cultural reformation.

Through training, co-creation, and exchange, participants build cross-cultural communication skills, teamwork, and a global perspective — while bringing light and hope to others through practical action.

02 — Program

Three Core Pillars

Cultural Exchange × Character Education × Teamwork — the framework guiding every activity of Oli-Action.

A / Exchange

Taiwan Cultural Exchange

Interactive Chinese learning, cultural riddles, and hands-on crafts that build friendship between Taiwanese and Indian youth.

B / Training

Volunteer Training

Led by Taiwanese facilitators — team-building, lesson rehearsals, and practical simulations that grow your leadership and spirit of service.

C / Service

School Camp Service

Taiwanese and Indian volunteers join hands to visit Government Schools, inspiring children through character games and team challenges.
