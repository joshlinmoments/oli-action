Cultural Exchange, Hand in Hand

The Baiyangshan Charitable Foundation, a non-profit from Taiwan, is guided by the mission of purifying minds & inspiring cultural reformation. Together with students from SIST, we form a joint international volunteer team to serve children at local Government Schools in India — building cross-cultural friendship, teamwork, and a global perspective while bringing light and hope through action.
